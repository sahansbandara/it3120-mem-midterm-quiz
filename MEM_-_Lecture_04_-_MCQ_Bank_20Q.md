## Q1

**Lecture:** Lecture 04 - Introduction to Human Resource Management  
**Type:** Single Choice

### Question
Which best describes Human Resource Management?

### Options
- A. Managing company finances
- B. Managing people strategically to achieve organizational goals
- C. Managing technology systems
- D. Managing customer relationships

### Correct Answer
B. Managing people strategically to achieve organizational goals

### 💡 Hint
Which option treats employees as key organizational assets and links their efforts to business goals?

### Explanation
HRM is a strategic and coherent approach to managing people as key organizational assets and aligning employee efforts with organizational goals.

### Why Other Answers Are Wrong
- A. Managing company finances belongs to Finance/Accounting.
- C. Managing technology systems is an IT-management function.
- D. Managing customer relationships belongs to CRM.

### ⚫ Trap

## Q2

**Lecture:** Lecture 04 - Introduction to Human Resource Management  
**Type:** Single Choice

### Question
What is the main purpose of recruitment?

### Options
- A. To reduce employee workload
- B. To train employees
- C. To create a pool of qualified candidates
- D. To evaluate employee performance

### Correct Answer
C. To create a pool of qualified candidates

### 💡 Hint
Recruitment happens before selection. What does it need to create first?

### Explanation
Recruitment identifies and attracts potential candidates so the organization has a **pool of qualified candidates** for current and future workforce needs.

### Why Other Answers Are Wrong
- A. Reducing workload is not the primary recruitment purpose.
- B. Training is part of Learning & Development.
- D. Evaluating employee performance is Performance Management.

### ⚫ Trap
**Recruitment builds the pool; Selection picks from the pool.**
