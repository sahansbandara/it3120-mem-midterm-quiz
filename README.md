# Y03S01 Quiz and Paper

This repository contains interactive practice papers for Year 3 Semester 1 modules. Each paper runs directly in a web browser and provides automatic marking, progress tracking, and answer feedback. Papers are separated by module below.

## IT3120 — Modern Enterprise Management (MEM)

### Paper 1: Interactive Midterm Practice

This paper creates focused practice sessions from a bank of 120 questions. It is suitable for repeated midterm practice because students can generate a new mixed paper or concentrate on one lecture.

**Inside this paper:**

- 120-question bank covering Lectures 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, and 07
- 30-question mixed midterm paper
- 120-question full-bank mode
- 20-question single-lecture mode
- Single-choice and multiple-answer questions
- Hints, live score, progress tracking, and answer explanations
- Wrong-answer feedback and partial-credit scoring for multiple-answer questions

**Open paper:** [MEM Interactive Midterm Practice](https://sahansbandara.github.io/Y03S01-Quiz-and-Paper/MEM/index.html)

### Paper 2: Comprehensive Exam Prep Question Bank

This paper displays the complete MEM question bank and allows students to revise all questions together or filter them by lecture. It is useful for structured revision and checking understanding topic by topic.

**Inside this paper:**

- 120 questions divided into six lecture sections
- Lecture 01: Management
- Lecture 02: Marketing 6.0
- Lecture 03: Economics
- Lecture 04: Human Resource Management
- Lecture 05: Accounting
- Lecture 07: Legal and Ethical Issues
- Multiple-choice and multiple-answer questions
- Instant correct or incorrect feedback with answer rationale
- Answered count, correct count, and accuracy percentage

**Open paper:** [MEM Comprehensive Exam Prep](https://sahansbandara.github.io/Y03S01-Quiz-and-Paper/MEM/Quiz.html)

## SE3100 — Architecture Based Development (ABD)

### Paper 1: Interactive Midterm and Exam Practice

This configurable paper is designed for both short revision sessions and complete ABD exam practice. Students can choose a mixed paper, focus on one lecture, or attempt the full question bank.

**Inside this paper:**

- 100-question bank with 20 questions from each lecture
- Lecture 01: Introduction to Software Architecture
- Lecture 02: Quality Attributes, Scenarios, and Tactics
- Lecture 03: Architectural Thinking
- Lecture 04: Monolithic Architectural Styles
- Lecture 05: Distributed Architectural Styles
- Mixed papers containing 10, 20, 30, 40, or 50 questions
- Single-lecture and full 100-question modes
- Single-choice and multiple-answer questions
- Optional configurable timer
- Hints, question navigation, flagging, progress tracking, and partial-credit scoring
- Correct-answer explanations, wrong-answer analysis, and exam-trap notes

**Open paper:** [ABD Interactive Midterm and Exam Practice](https://sahansbandara.github.io/Y03S01-Quiz-and-Paper/ABD/SE3100_Interactive_Exam_Quiz.html)
