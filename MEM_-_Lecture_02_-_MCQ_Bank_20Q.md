## Q1

**Lecture:** Lecture 02 - Introduction to Marketing  
**Type:** Single Choice

### Question
What should be the first step before launching a product?

### Options
- A. Advertising campaign
- B. Market research
- C. Pricing strategy
- D. Product launch

### Correct Answer
B. Market research

### 💡 Hint
Before deciding price, promotion, or launch, what should the company learn about customers first?

### Explanation
Market research identifies customer needs, preferences, and behaviour and reduces uncertainty before marketing decisions are made.

### Why Other Answers Are Wrong
- A. Advertising depends on information gathered from research.
- C. Pricing decisions should be informed by market and customer research.
- D. Product launch is the outcome, not the first step.

### ⚫ Trap
Research comes before the execution decisions.

## Q2

**Lecture:** Lecture 02 - Introduction to Marketing  
**Type:** Single Choice

### Question
Which strategy focuses on **selecting** the right customer group?

### Options
- A. Positioning
- B. Segmentation
- C. Targeting
- D. Branding

### Correct Answer
C. Targeting

### 💡 Hint
Remember the STP verbs: divide → select → shape perception.

### Explanation
Segmentation divides the market, Targeting selects the most attractive segment, and Positioning creates a desired image in that segment's mind.

### Why Other Answers Are Wrong
- A. Positioning shapes perception after the target is chosen.
- B. Segmentation creates groups but does not select one.
- D. Branding is not one of the three STP stages.

### ⚫ Trap
**Segmentation = divide; Targeting = select; Positioning = perception.**

## Q9

**Lecture:** Lecture 02 - Introduction to Marketing  
**Type:** Single Choice

### Question
Reports and existing databases are an example of:

### Options
- A. Primary data
- B. Secondary data
- C. Digital metrics
- D. Brand equity

### Correct Answer
B. Secondary data

### 💡 Hint
Ask whether the information was collected directly for the current research or already existed.

### Explanation
Secondary data is information that already exists, such as reports and databases. Primary data is collected directly through methods such as surveys and interviews.

### Why Other Answers Are Wrong
- A. Primary data is collected directly for the research purpose.
- C. Digital metrics measure performance and are not the data-source category asked here.
- D. Brand equity is the value associated with customer perceptions of a brand.

### ⚫ Trap
**Who originally collected the data** determines primary vs secondary.
