## Q1

**Lecture:** Lecture 03 - Introduction to Economics  
**Type:** Single Choice

### Question
Opportunity cost refers to:

### Options
- A. Total cost of production
- B. The next best alternative forgone
- C. Monetary cost only
- D. Fixed cost

### Correct Answer
B. The next best alternative forgone

### 💡 Hint
Think about what you give up when choosing one option instead of another.

### Explanation
Opportunity cost is the value of the **next-best alternative** given up when a choice is made.

### Why Other Answers Are Wrong
- A. Total cost of production is a different cost concept.
- C. Opportunity cost can involve time, benefits, output, or money.
- D. Fixed cost is a production-cost category.

### ⚫ Trap
It is the **next-best** alternative forgone, not all alternatives.

## Q11

**Lecture:** Lecture 03 - Introduction to Economics  
**Type:** Single Choice

### Question
A firm has total fixed costs of $3,000 and total variable costs of $5,000 while producing 400 units. Cost per unit is:

### Options
- A. $7.50
- B. $12.50
- C. $20.00
- D. $2.00

### Correct Answer
C. $20.00

### 💡 Hint
Combine fixed and variable costs before dividing by units.

### Explanation
Cost per unit = (TFC + TVC) / Units  
= ($3,000 + $5,000) / 400  
= $8,000 / 400  
= **$20.00**

### Why Other Answers Are Wrong
- A. Uses fixed cost only.
- B. Uses variable cost only.
- D. Is an incorrect calculation.

### ⚫ Trap
Use **total cost**, not only one cost component.

## Q12

**Lecture:** Lecture 03 - Introduction to Economics  
**Type:** Single Choice

### Question
Using the coffee-shop table: total cost at Q=5 is $6.50 and at Q=6 is $7.80. Marginal cost of the 6th cup is:

### Options
- A. $1.30
- B. $1.10
- C. $0.50
- D. $7.80

### Correct Answer
A. $1.30

### 💡 Hint
Use **MC = ΔTC / ΔQ**.

### Explanation
MC = ($7.80 − $6.50) / (6 − 5)  
= $1.30 / 1  
= **$1.30**

### Why Other Answers Are Wrong
- B. Is not the change from Q=5 to Q=6.
- C. Is not the marginal cost of the 6th cup.
- D. Is total cost, not marginal cost.

### ⚫ Trap
Do not use **TC/Q** for marginal cost.

## Q20

**Lecture:** Lecture 03 - Introduction to Economics  
**Type:** Multiple Answer

### Question
Which statements about **market equilibrium, surplus, and shortage** are correct? (Select all that apply.)

### Options
- A. At equilibrium, quantity supplied equals quantity demanded
- B. A surplus puts downward pressure on price
- C. A shortage puts upward pressure on price
- D. A surplus occurs when quantity demanded exceeds quantity supplied
- E. Market equilibrium is the price at which the market clears

### Correct Answers
A, B, C, E

### 💡 Hint
Compare **Qs** with **Qd**, then remember how price responds.

### Explanation
At equilibrium, **Qs = Qd** and the market clears. A surplus pushes prices down, while a shortage pushes prices up.

### Why Other Answers Are Wrong
- D. A surplus occurs when **Qs > Qd**. If **Qd > Qs**, there is a shortage.

### ⚫ Trap
⚠ **Over-selection warning:** **Surplus = Qs > Qd; Shortage = Qd > Qs.**
