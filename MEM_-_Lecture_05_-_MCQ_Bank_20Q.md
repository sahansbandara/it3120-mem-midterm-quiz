## Q1

**Lecture:** Lecture 05 - Introduction to Finance Accounting  
**Type:** Single Choice

### Question
Which statement shows financial position at a specific point in time?

### Options
- A. Income Statement
- B. Balance Sheet
- C. Cash Flow Statement
- D. Profit and Loss Statement

### Correct Answer
B. Balance Sheet

### 💡 Hint
Look for the statement that gives a **snapshot at one date**, not performance over a period.

### Explanation
The Balance Sheet shows the company’s financial position at a **specific point in time**, including assets, liabilities, and equity.

### Why Other Answers Are Wrong
- A. Income Statement shows financial performance over a period.
- C. Cash Flow Statement shows movement of cash over a period.
- D. Profit and Loss Statement is another name for the Income Statement.

### ⚫ Trap
**“Point in time” → Balance Sheet. “Over a period” → Income Statement/Cash Flow.**

## Q11

### Question
Revenue LKR 100,000; Cost of Sales LKR 60,000; Operating Expenses LKR 20,000. Gross Profit is:

### Options
- A. 20,000
- B. 40,000
- C. 60,000
- D. 80,000

### Correct Answer
B. 40,000

### 💡 Hint
Gross Profit subtracts **Cost of Sales**, not Operating Expenses.

### Explanation
Gross Profit = Revenue − Cost of Sales  
= 100,000 − 60,000  
= **LKR 40,000**

### Why Other Answers Are Wrong
- A. This is operating profit after deducting operating expenses.
- C. This is the Cost of Sales figure.
- D. This subtracts only Operating Expenses from Revenue.

### ⚫ Trap
Operating Expenses are deducted **after** Gross Profit.

Same company: Assets LKR 150,000; Liabilities LKR 90,000. Equity is:

### Options
- A. 60,000
- B. 90,000
- C. 150,000
- D. 240,000

### Correct Answer
A. 60,000

### 💡 Hint
Use **Equity = Assets − Liabilities**.

- B. Repeats the liabilities figure.
- C. Repeats the assets figure.

### ⚫ Trap

## Q15

**Lecture:** Lecture 05 - Introduction to Finance Accounting  
**Type:** Single Choice

### Question
Same data. Net Increase in Cash across all activities:

### Options
- A. +20,000
- B. +30,000
- C. +50,000
- D. +80,000

### Correct Answer
B. +30,000

### 💡 Hint
Combine Operating, Investing, and Financing net cash flows.

### Explanation
Operating = 80,000 − 50,000 = **+30,000**  
Investing = **−20,000**  
Financing = 30,000 − 10,000 = **+20,000**  
Net Increase = 30,000 − 20,000 + 20,000 = **+LKR 30,000**

### Why Other Answers Are Wrong
- A. Does not include all three categories correctly.
- C. Omits some outflows.
- D. Uses the customer inflow only.

### ⚫ Trap
Operating net and total net both equal 30,000 here **by coincidence**.
