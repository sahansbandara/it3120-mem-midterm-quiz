## Q10

**Lecture:** Lecture 01 - Introduction to Management Concepts  
**Type:** Single Choice

### Question
In Herzberg's theory, salary is classified as:

### Options
- A. A motivator
- B. A hygiene factor
- C. Both
- D. Neither

### Correct Answer
B. A hygiene factor

### 💡 Hint
Which factors prevent dissatisfaction rather than create deep satisfaction?

### Explanation
Herzberg classifies salary as a **hygiene factor**. Hygiene factors reduce dissatisfaction but are not the main source of strong motivation.

### Why Other Answers Are Wrong
- A. Motivators include achievement, recognition, responsibility, growth, and meaningful work.
- C. The theory separates hygiene factors and motivators.
- D. Salary is explicitly included among hygiene factors.

### ⚫ Trap
Salary can reduce dissatisfaction without being a true **motivator** in Herzberg's model.
